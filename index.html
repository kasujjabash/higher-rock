Our Heart

Faithful with what we’ve been given

The Higher Rock Foundation is a family foundation. Each year we set aside a portion of what we’ve been entrusted with and give it away — to ministries and leaders we know, trust, and have come to love.

We believe that everything we have is a gift, held in trust for a season. So we try to be faithful with it: giving generously, giving patiently, and staying close enough to the work to celebrate what God is doing through the people on the ground.

We are not the story here. The leaders we support are — and the families and communities whose lives are being changed for generations to come.

A Note on Giving

We give through relationship

Our partnerships grow out of trust built over time, and our giving is directed to a small group of organizations we know personally.

For that reason, we do not accept unsolicited grant applications or proposals.

We say this gently, and with real respect for the many worthy causes in the world. If your work and ours seem deeply aligned, you are welcome to introduce yourself below — but please know we are not able to respond to most inquiries, and a message is never a guarantee of support.
